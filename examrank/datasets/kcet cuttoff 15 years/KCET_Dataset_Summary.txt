📈 TREND LOGIC
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
The dataset shows realistic trends:
• UVCE CSE (2024, R1, GM): Rank ~5,600
• UVCE CSE (2015, R1, GM): Rank ~8,700 (more lenient, fewer candidates)
• UVCE CSE (2010, R1, GM): Rank ~10,300 (most lenient, fewest candidates)

This reflects:
✓ Growing engineering demand and competition over 15 years
✓ Increasing KCET participation year-on-year
✓ Tightening cutoffs for premier colleges
✓ Reserved categories maintain consistent ratio relative to GM

🔧 HOW TO USE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Filter by:
  • Year: df[df['Year'] == 2024]
  • College: df[df['College_Code'] == 'E001']
  • Course: df[df['Course'].str.contains('COMPUTER SCIENCE')]
  • Category: df[df['Category'] == 'GM']
  • Round: df[df['Round'] == 'R1']
  • Cutoff range: df[(df['Cutoff_Rank'] > 1000) & (df['Cutoff_Rank'] < 5000)]

Combine filters:
  df[(df['Year']==2024) & (df['College_Code']=='E001') & (df['Category']=='GM')]

Export to Excel/JSON:
  df.to_excel('kcet_analysis.xlsx')
  df.to_json('kcet_data.json')
